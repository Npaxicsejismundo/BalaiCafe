Balai — the house

A Cebuano house, open from first coffee to last call.

Balai opens at six in the morning with brewed coffee and garlic rice, and stays on through lechon kawali, kare-kare and halo-halo until midnight. Long tables, warm wood, a garden that catches the afternoon light.

Everything comes out of one kitchen and one bakery, the way food arrives on a Sunday — all at once, for everyone.

Kaon ta, bai!

Under one roof

Four rooms, one house

Balai Brunch

Silogs, tapa cheese steak and ube french toast, served daily from 6 to 11 in the morning.

The Bakery

House-made pandesal, cookies, cinnamon rolls and cakes, baked through the morning.

The Basement

Downstairs after dark — beer, pulutan and a room built for staying late.

Farooj

Charcoal chicken, kebab and wraps with curry rice and four house sauces.
